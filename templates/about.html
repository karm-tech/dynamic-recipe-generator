🚀 What We Built in DRG 5.0

With DRG 5.0, users can:

  • 🍽 Search for recipes by ingredient or name with dietary filters
  • ❤️ Save favorite recipes for quick access
  • 📜 Auto-tracked recipe history with timestamps
  • 🧠 AI-powered 7-day meal planner with dietary preferences
  • 📥 Download recipes as beautifully formatted PDFs
  • 🛍️ Auto-generated shopping lists from recipes
  • 🎉 Explore recipes based on seasonal festivals
  • 🔥 Smart accessory suggestions for your kitchen

🔮 What's Coming in DRG 6.0

  • 🗣️ Voice-Activated Cooking Assistant – Hands-free control with voice commands.
  • 🔐 User Login System – Personal profiles with saved preferences and full database backend.
  • 📧 Recipe Scheduler – Schedule recipes to be sent to your email or phone.
  • 🏆 Recipe Ratings & Reviews – Rate and review recipes from the community.
  • 📱 Mobile App – Native iOS and Android applications.
  • 💾 Advanced Database Integration – PostgreSQL backend with user data persistence.
